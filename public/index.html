Local AI

How Local AI works

A private chat app that runs on your computer. You ask a question, and a model answers in plain language, adding charts, timelines, diagrams, steps and key numbers when they explain something better than words. Your chats and files stay on this computer. The model that answers is either one on this computer, or a cloud model from a provider you have a key for.

What you need to do

  1. Add a provider key

    Cloud models need a key from the company that runs them. Infero is the main provider, and is run by HeyElsa, which also makes Local AI. Sign in at console.infero.xyz, create a key (it starts with sk-infero-) and paste it into Settings. You only need one key to start. Others, such as MiniRouter, OpenRouter, Groq or Together AI, can be added later under More providers. Keys are saved in this app’s .env file, readable only by your user account, and never sent to the page.

  2. Add MiniRouter or OpenRouter for web search

    Infero can’t search the web. With a MiniRouter or OpenRouter key, questions that need current information are searched there instead, and the line under the message box says so before you send. MiniRouter also makes videos.

  3. Run models on this computer

    For answers that never leave this computer, install Ollama and open it. Then press the model name next to the send button and choose Download a local model. gemma3:4b is about 3.3 GB and can see images.

  4. Ask

    Type your question and press Enter (Shift+Enter adds a new line), or press the microphone and speak. The model name next to the send button chooses who answers.

What you can do

Ask, Image, Video
Choose what to make under the message box. Image and Video show an estimated cost before you make anything.
Attach
Use the paper clip, paste, or drag images and videos in. A video is sent as a few still frames, without sound.
Web search
The globe turns search on (orange) or off. A model searches only when it needs to.
Voice
The microphone writes down what you say and asks it. Read aloud under an answer reads it back.
Thinking
Some models share their reasoning. It appears above the answer and folds away when the answer starts.
Chats
Your chats are listed on the left. Find one by title, delete one, or delete them all.

Where your messages go

The line under the message box always says where your next message goes, before you send it. “Stays on this computer” means a local model. Anything else names the provider. Some providers, such as MiniRouter and OpenRouter, pass your message on to the company whose model answers. Infero, which is run by HeyElsa, passes it to the host it picks. Groq, Cerebras, Together AI, Fireworks and DeepInfra run the models themselves. Voice goes to the provider chosen in Settings, even when you use a local model.

Privacy controls

Private mode
In the sidebar. Blocks every cloud request, so only local models answer, and saves nothing new.
Temporary chat
Not saved or listed. Its files are erased when you leave it.
Hide personal details
On by default. Email addresses, phone, card and bank numbers and API keys you type are replaced with placeholders before a cloud model sees them, then restored in the answer. Pattern matching can miss things.
Passcode and lock
Always on. Your passcode encrypts chats and files on disk and is needed to use the app. It locks on restart, when idle, or when you press Lock. A forgotten passcode cannot be recovered.
Delete automatically
In Settings. Removes chats you haven’t used for 1 to 90 days.

Costs

Cloud providers charge your account with them, not this app. Each model in the picker shows its price, Image and Video show an estimate first, and your remaining balance appears at the bottom of the sidebar when the provider reports it. Local models are free to run.

Terms, privacy and license

Settings

Image mode shows only this provider’s image models. Automatic shows every provider’s.

Used when web search is on and your model’s provider can’t search, such as Infero. MiniRouter and OpenRouter models always search with their own provider.

Privacy

Email addresses, phone numbers, card and bank numbers, US Social Security numbers and API keys you type are replaced before sending and restored in the answer. Pattern matching can miss things, and names, addresses, images and voice are not covered.

Passcode and lock

Chats and files are encrypted on disk, and the app can be used only after you enter your passcode. It locks when it restarts. A forgotten passcode cannot be recovered.

Where your data goes

Chats, attachments and generated files stay on this computer, in the app's data folder. Deleting a chat deletes its files. The page itself connects only to the app on this computer. The line under the message box always shows where your next message goes.

Private mode saves nothing new: chats live only in the open page and attachments only in memory, and both are erased when you leave the chat. Your settings and the chats you saved earlier stay.

Models on this computer answer through Ollama, and your messages stay here. Private mode blocks every cloud request in the app's server, including web search, Image, Video and voice input. Read aloud then uses your computer's own voices. Downloading a model contacts Ollama's library, but sends none of your chats.

Cloud models receive your message and attachments through the provider named under the message box. For attached videos, only sampled still frames are sent.

Infero, the main provider, is run by HeyElsa, which also makes Local AI. What you send to Infero models goes to HeyElsa and is covered by Infero’s privacy policy, which says it does not store the content of requests by default and does not train on it. The companies that host its models receive your messages under their own policies. Infero cannot search the web, so with web search on your question goes to MiniRouter or OpenRouter instead, and the line under the message box says so.

MiniRouter keeps prompts and responses in private debug traces for 30 days, with no opt-out. OpenRouter and each model's provider have their own policies.

Voice outside Private mode goes to the first provider you have a key for, in the order Infero, MiniRouter, OpenRouter, even with a local model. Recordings are not saved by this app. With a cloud model, read aloud goes there too.
